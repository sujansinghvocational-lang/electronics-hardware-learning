Basic Electronics

Electronics is the branch of science that deals with the flow of electrons and electronic components.

Resistor

A resistor limits the flow of current in an electrical circuit. Unit: Ohm (Ω).

Capacitor

A capacitor stores electrical energy. Unit: Farad (F).

Diode

A diode allows current to flow in only one direction.

Transistor

A transistor is used as a switch and amplifier.
